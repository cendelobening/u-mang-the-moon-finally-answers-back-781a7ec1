The Moon finally answers back. After four billion years of wishes, howls and 2 a.m. promises, it has notes — and it is not impressed:

> I've listened to every wish, every howl, every 2 a.m. promise — and honestly, most of you should aim lower. I'm a rock, not your therapist.

Why it should win: it names every habit the Moon is answering, then turns the staring back on the humans twice — first grading their wishes ("aim lower"), then refusing the job they keep assigning it ("not your therapist"). The laugh lands on one read, in the voice of a weary rock that never asked to be anyone's wish-granter.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`) — one line, no trailing newline.
